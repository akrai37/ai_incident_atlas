About this view

  • Sample of 480 incidents selected across five study sectors: transportation, media/content, public safety, healthcare, and finance.
  • Claude extracted structured pathway annotations from incident reports. A 30-incident human-validation procedure is prepared for final verification; its results are not yet reported here.
  • Not documented means the extraction did not identify a clearly documented prior warning or skipped check in the source report; it does not prove that prevention was impossible.
  • AIID reporting coverage may overrepresent English-language, US/EU, or publicly reported consumer-facing incidents; this view inherits those limitations.
